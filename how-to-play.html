How to play Capiclue

Capiclue is a free daily capital city game built on fun facts. Every day there are five mystery capitals. Read a surprising fact, guess the city, and score more the fewer facts you need.

Play today’s game →

The basics

  1. Read the first fact about a mystery capital city, for example “Grace Kelly became its princess in 1956.”
  2. Type your guess. As you type, a list of capitals appears to choose from. A wrong guess reveals the next fact.
  3. Each city has up to four clues. Three fun facts, getting easier as you go, and finally the country it is the capital of.
  4. Five cities a day. A new set appears at midnight, wherever you are.

Points

The sooner you guess, the more you score. The best possible day is 100 points.

Three levels

  • Easy: famous capitals. Pick the answer from four choices.
  • Medium: well-known capitals. Type your answer; the continent and first letter are shown.
  • Hard: all 193 capitals. Type your answer, with only the continent shown.

Each level has its own five cities every day, and the same capital never shows the same facts on two levels on the same day. On Medium and Hard, 🍀 Lucky guess picks a random capital for you: if it’s right, you get the full points for that fact.

Streaks and your stats

Finish a game every day to build your 🔥 streak. Tap “Your stats” on the home or results screen to see your days played, current and best streak, average and best score, and how your scores spread out.

Play with friends: leagues

Tap the 🏆 trophy to create a private league and share the invite link with friends, family or colleagues. When you create one, you choose:

  • Which days count: every day, or Monday to Friday only (great for office leagues).
  • How long a round lasts: 1 week, 2 weeks or 30 days. The highest total at the end wins, and anyone can start a new round.

League points reward harder levels: Easy ×1, Medium ×2, Hard ×3. Leagues only need a nickname. Save your 🔑 backup code so you can always get back to your nickname on a new browser.

Questions

What time do new cities appear?
At midnight on your own device’s clock, wherever you are in the world. The home screen also shows a countdown to the next set.
Is Capiclue free?
Yes. There is nothing to buy and no sign-up. It works in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer.
Do I need an account?
No. Your progress and stats are saved on your device. Leagues only need a nickname: no email or password.
Can I play all three levels?
Yes. Easy, Medium and Hard each have their own five cities every day, so you can play one or all three.
Can I play earlier days?
Not at the moment. Every day is a fresh set of five, so come back tomorrow for new cities.
How do streaks work?
Finish at least one game a day and your streak grows. Miss a day and it starts again, but your best streak is kept in “Your stats”.
Can I play on my phone and my laptop?
Yes. In the Leagues panel, tap “Play on another device” to get a short code or QR code for your other device. A backup code lets you get back in if a browser ever forgets you.
Is there an app?
You can add Capiclue to your home screen from your browser’s Share or menu button. It then opens like an app and works even with a weak connection.
