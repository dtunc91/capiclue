Daily geography games worth playing

Loved Wordle and want something for geography fans? These free daily games each take a few minutes, and each tests something different: shapes, maps, borders or capitals. Here is a short, honest guide, including our own game.

Capiclue: guess the capital from fun facts

Best for: trivia fans, and learning something new even when you miss

Instead of maps or flags, Capiclue gives you surprising facts about a mystery capital: a city with a Museum of Broken Relationships, a capital whose giant Ferris wheel has turned since 1897. Five cities a day, three levels (Easy, Medium, Hard), streaks, and private leagues to play with friends or your team.

Play Capiclue →

Worldle: guess the country from its outline.

You see the silhouette of a country and have six guesses. Each wrong guess tells you how far away and in which direction the answer is. Good if you know your maps and shapes.

Play Worldle ↗

Globle: find the mystery country on a globe.

Guess any country and it lights up on a 3D globe: the warmer the colour, the closer you are. There is no limit on guesses, so it is relaxed and great for learning where countries are.

Play Globle ↗

Travle: travel between two countries over land.

You are given a start and end country and must name the countries in between to make the shortest route. A fun test of which countries border which.

Play Travle ↗

Capitalle: find the mystery capital with distance hints.

Guess a capital city in six tries, with distance and direction hints after each guess. A good pick if you like capitals and map-style clues.

Play Capitalle ↗

Playing with friends or colleagues

Most daily games let you share your result as a grid of coloured squares. Capiclue also has built-in leagues: create one, share the link, and everyone’s daily scores go into one table. You can make it Monday to Friday for the office, pick a round of 1 week, 2 weeks or 30 days, and see who wins. See how Capiclue works.

The other games listed here are run by their own creators; we are not connected to them.
