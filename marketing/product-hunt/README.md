# Product Hunt launch: Capiclue

**Planned launch:** Sunday 15 November 2026, going live at 12:01 am US Pacific time (8:01 am UK time).
This folder is kept off the website (see `.assetsignore`).

## Listing

- **Name:** Capiclue
- **Link:** https://capiclue.com/?r=producthunt
- **Tagline** (60 characters max): Guess the capital city from fun facts, daily
- **Topics:** Games, Education, Web App

**Description**

> A free daily geography game. Read a surprising fact about a mystery capital and guess the city: the fewer facts you need, the more points you get. Five new capitals every day, three levels (Easy, Medium, Hard), streaks, and private leagues to play with friends or your team. No sign-up, no download.

**First comment (from the maker)**

> Hi Product Hunt 👋 I built Capiclue because most capital games are about maps and flags. I wanted one where you *learn* something even when you miss. Our office plays it in a league every weekday; the Mon–Fri leagues and points multipliers came from their feedback. Would love to hear which fact surprised you most!

## Short blurb for directories

> Capiclue is a free daily capital city game built on fun facts. Guess five mystery capitals from trivia clues, keep your streak going, and compete with friends in private leagues. Works on any phone or computer.

Where: AlternativeTo (as an alternative to Worldle and Globle), daily-game and "Worldle alternatives" lists, Reddit r/geography and r/dailygames.

## Launch checklist

1. During the week before: create the Product Hunt account and use it a little (upvote and comment on a few products).
2. Tell friends, colleagues and the Marketplace Ops league in advance: ask them to have a look and leave a comment on launch day. Don't ask directly for upvotes; Product Hunt discourages it.
3. On the day: post the maker comment straight away and reply to every comment, especially in the first few hours.
4. Afterwards: check capiclue.com/stats → "Where visitors come from" (Product Hunt visits show up as "Shared link: producthunt").
