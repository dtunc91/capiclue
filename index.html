About Capiclue

Capiclue is a free daily city guessing game where every clue is a fun fact. Instead of maps or flags, you get surprising trivia: a city with a Museum of Broken Relationships, a capital whose giant Ferris wheel has turned since 1897, a place nicknamed the “Jerusalem of Europe”. Can you name the city?

Each day brings five mystery capitals from Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas and Oceania, with up to four facts each, starting tricky and getting easier. Even when you miss one, you walk away knowing something new about the world.

No sign-up, no download: it works on any phone, tablet or computer.
